How It Works

  1. Rainwater is collected from the roof.
  2. Collected water passes through a filter.
  3. Flowing/falling water rotates a small turbine.
  4. The turbine drives a generator.
  5. Generated electricity is stored in a battery.
  6. The battery can power a small heating element.
  7. Solar thermal energy directly heats water.
  8. Both sources contribute to the same insulated hot-water tank.
  9. A controller decides which source should operate based on sunlight and rainfall.

Proposed Innovation

This student prototype focuses on combining two complementary, already-known renewable techniques into one compact, automatically-managed system:

  • Automatic source switching between solar and rain-recovered energy
  • Efficient rainwater routing from roof to turbine
  • Compact, integrated hardware design
  • Combining solar thermal heating with rain-energy recovery
  • Using rain-generated electricity as supplementary heating
  • One common insulated water tank for both sources
  • Low-cost, buildable prototype design
